Privacy Policy
Effective date: October 7, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains what data Ryntra (“we”, “us”) collects through ryntra.io, the Ryntra cabinet, the Ryntra app for Android and the Ryntra Telegram bot, how we use it, and the choices you have. Our approach is data minimalism: we collect only what the product needs to work, and the product needs remarkably little — your account is your wallet.
1. Data we collect
Wallet address (public) — when you connect a wallet. A wallet address is public blockchain information; it lets us show what the wallet holds, prepare transactions for it and keep the record of the operations you make through Ryntra. We never ask for seed phrases, private keys or wallet passwords.
Sign-in signature and sessions — when you sign in with your wallet. Signing in is a message your wallet signs; we keep the signature’s proof, the account it created for that address, and server-side session records (issue and expiry times, last activity, revocation state) so that sessions can be managed, and ended on one device or on all of them. There is no email, password or social sign-in for customers.
Operations and receipts — the swaps, trades, bridges, transfers and rewards claims you make through Ryntra: what you reviewed, what the network did, the transaction signatures and their status. This is the Activity history and the receipts you can open there.
Rewards, Points and referrals — the ledgers that record cashback and referral shares accrued to your wallet, your Points and level, your referral code, the wallet that invited you and the wallets you invited, and the claims you made.
Preferences kept on the account — your Spot watchlist. Language, theme and the Simple/Pro depth are kept in your browser only and are not sent to us.
Telegram channel data (optional) — if you connect Telegram as a notification channel from Settings, or interact with the bot: your Telegram identifier and the basic profile data Telegram supplies, such as your username, name and interface language, and the messages you send the bot. Telegram is a notification channel, not a way to sign in.
Correspondence and feedback — if you email us or use the feedback form, we process your message and any details you include in order to answer you or act on it, and we keep the thread while it is needed for that. We do not operate an email waitlist.
First-party cookies — a session cookie for the signed-in account, short-lived sign-in state, a wallet-proof cookie when you request proof of ownership, and a preference cookie for the public site’s language. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
Usage events — we record page and call-to-action events in our own database to understand how the product is used. Each event may include the event name, the placement and locale, the page path, and the referring URL (truncated). We also use Vercel Analytics for aggregate traffic. None of this is used to build individual advertising profiles or sold.
2. The Ryntra app for Android
The Ryntra app for Android works with the same account as the website, and section 1 applies to your account in the app too. What is different on the phone:
What reaches Ryntra — the public address of the wallet you connect — to show what it holds and, once you sign in with it, your own plans, rules and notes. The app gets its data from ryntra.io; asset logos load from the public addresses where they are published, and those servers see your phone's IP address.
What stays on your phone — your session, in the phone's secure storage; your watchlist; the last Home screen you saw (public prices and the time); a short record of the steps you took in the app, with nothing that identifies you; your preferences — language, theme, hidden amounts. Signing out deletes your session and the private data kept on the phone; removing the app deletes everything.
Your keys — every signature happens in the wallet app on your phone, through Mobile Wallet Adapter; your seed phrase and private keys never reach the Ryntra app.
The push token, only when you turn phone notifications on — then the phone's push token, the app's version, your language, your quiet hours and the kind of notes the phone takes (changes to your plans) are stored with your account, and the notes are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. Turning phone notifications off or signing out removes this phone's registration from your account. While they are off, no push token is sent to Ryntra.
Camera (CAMERA) — asked only when you choose to scan or to take a photo — to scan a wallet address or a payment QR code, and to take a picture for a token you launch. Nothing is recorded; a picture you take is sent to Ryntra only when you press to launch a token with it, and from then on it is at a public address even if you do not sign.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — declared for phone notifications — reminders and the state of your operations. The current version (1.11.0) has no phone notifications and does not ask for it; notes stay in the app's inbox. A version that brings phone notifications asks only when you turn them on.
What the app does not ask for — access to your photos or files, your location, contacts, microphone, accounts or advertising identifier; it carries no third-party analytics or advertising library. You can refuse the camera or notifications, or take them back in the phone's settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.
A token you launch — its picture can also be chosen in the phone's system photo picker, which hands the app only that one image, with no permission to your photos or files. The picture, name, ticker, description and links of a token you launch become public together with the token: anyone can see them on Ryntra's pages, in the app and at the public address the token names on the blockchain. Put nothing in them you do not want to be public. What a launched token may not carry is in the Terms of Use.
3. Data we never collect
Seed phrases, wallet private keys, wallet passwords, or any credential that controls funds.
Deposits or funds of any kind — Ryntra is non-custodial by design.
Payment or card data — there is nothing to pay Ryntra; its service fee is part of the on-chain transaction your wallet signs.
An email address or password for a customer account — the wallet is the account.
4. How we use data
To provide the cabinet: read what your wallet holds, prepare and simulate the transactions you ask for, and record their results as Activity and receipts.
To run the Rewards and Points programme: accrue cashback and referral shares on collected fees, count Points, and pay claims to the wallet you are signed in with.
To send the notifications you asked for through the Telegram channel you connected, or to your phone if you turn phone notifications on.
To respond to you, prevent abuse, and keep the Service secure (including rate limiting and session management).
To understand aggregate product usage and improve the Service.
We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.
5. Where data is processed
Your data is processed by the infrastructure and service providers we rely on to run the Service:
Supabase — database hosting for accounts and proven wallets, session records, operations and receipts, the Rewards, Points and referral ledgers, watchlists, notification channels, feedback and usage events.
Vercel — website hosting and privacy-friendly aggregate analytics.
Telegram — bot messaging for the notification channel you connect; your interaction is also subject to Telegram’s own privacy policy.
Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers the phone notifications you turn on in the Ryntra app for Android; it receives the phone’s push token and the note.
Blockchain and market providers — a Solana RPC provider and the liquidity, routing, bridging and market-data providers that price and execute your operations receive what the operation needs: your public wallet address and the transaction your wallet will sign. A transaction you sign is public on the blockchain by nature.
These providers process data on our behalf and under their own security and compliance programs. We do not transfer your data to anyone else, except where required by law.
6. Retention and deletion
We keep your data while your account is in use. You can request deletion of your account data at any time — email hello@ryntra.io from a way we can link to your account (for example, by mentioning the wallet address you sign in with; we may ask you to prove ownership with a signature). We will delete your account data within 30 days of a verified request. Transactions recorded on a public blockchain cannot be deleted by anyone, including us. Residual copies may persist in encrypted backups for a limited period before being purged.
7. Your rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@ryntra.io. We will respond within a reasonable time and in accordance with applicable law.
8. Children
The Service is not directed to persons under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a minor has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.
9. Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be announced in the Service.
