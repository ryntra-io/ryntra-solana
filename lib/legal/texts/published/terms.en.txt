5. Rewards, referrals and Points
When Ryntra receives its service fee on an eligible operation, a share of the fee it received — after any provider’s own share — is credited under the Rewards programme: 50% to the wallet that made the operation, 10–30% to the wallet that invited it, by that wallet’s rank when the operation settled, and 5% to the wallet that invited that one. Only a fee actually received by Ryntra is eligible; an operation on a route that carries no Ryntra fee, or a quoted fee that was not collected, earns nothing. Rewards are paid in USDC on Solana to the wallet you are signed in with, from a minimum of $1.00 USDC per claim, and are credited only after the operation has settled and its fee is confirmed. An inviter, once confirmed, cannot be changed; self-referral and circular referrals are refused. A wallet’s rank is set by its rank points over the last 30 days — one for each dollar of fee Ryntra received on the wallet’s own operations and on those of wallets it invited; rank points, like Points, have no cash value and cannot be transferred. Points record activity on the Service, have no cash value, cannot be transferred, and carry no promise of any token, airdrop or conversion. We may change, suspend or end the Rewards and Points programme; a change applies to operations made after it takes effect.
