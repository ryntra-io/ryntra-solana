Chapter 01

The city and its towers

Every coin on Solana is a tower. Tall means big, bright windows mean busy.

Imagine the whole Solana market as a city seen from a helicopter at night. Every coin is a building. A big coin is a tall skyscraper; a tiny new coin is a small house down by the docks. When many people trade a coin, its windows light up. When the price went up today, the roof glows green; when it fell, red.

You do not need to read a single chart to know what is going on: you look at the skyline.

Your steps
  1. Open ryntra.io. The city loads by itself, no sign-up.
  2. Pick a filter on top: All · Crypto · Memes · Stocks · New · Mine.
  3. Drag to turn the city, pinch or scroll to zoom.
  4. Tap any tower. Its window opens (chapter 3).
How it fits together
Under the hood
  • The city page is the site's home /: app/(home)/page.tsx/page.tsx>) renders CityApp from city/components/CityApp.tsx.
  • Market data comes from Jupiter's token API: readCity in city/lib/market/jupiter.ts, served live by app/api/city/route.ts.
  • buildTowers in city/lib/market/normalize.ts turns each coin into a tower and picks its district: core (SOL and the big coins), heights (DeFi), strip (memes), avenue (stocks and gold), docks (new pools). SOL stands in the middle as the spire.
  • towerHeight in city/lib/city/layout.ts: height follows market cap on a log scale, but never more than the coin's liquidity allows, so a coin with a big "paper" value and little real money stays short.
  • city/components/city/Towers.tsx: the roof turns mint at +5 % a day or more, red at −5 % or less, amber in between; window activity follows the number of traders in the last hour.
  • The camera and its gentle idle orbit live in city/components/city/CameraRig.tsx.
Honest notes
  • The pictures in this guide are real frames of ryntra.io, not drawings.
  • Towers show the market; a tall tower is not advice to buy it.
Chapter 02

Search and “Ask Ryntra”

Find any coin by name, or just ask the city a question in your own words.

Search is the city's map: type “BONK” and the camera flies to its tower. Ask Ryntra is a friendly guide standing next to you: you ask “what's rising and why?”, it looks at the market, answers in a few lines and lights up the towers it talks about. The guide explains; it never spends your money.

Your steps
  1. Tap the magnifier and type a ticker, a name or a coin address. Pick a result; the tower window opens.
  2. Or tap Ask Ryntra (on a computer: Ctrl K). Choose a ready question — Buy SOL · My portfolio · Launch a token · What's rising and why? — or type your own.
  3. Read the short answer. The towers in the answer glow; tap one to open it.
  4. Say what you want done — “buy SOL for 50” — and Ryntra prepares it for you to confirm (chapters 3 and 5).
How it fits together
Under the hood
  • Search box: city/components/search/CitySearch.tsx. Results are ranked by cityHits, launchHits, catalogHits and merged by mergeHits (8 best) in city/lib/search/rank.ts; sources are in city/lib/search/sources.ts.
  • Ask Ryntra: city/components/assistant/AssistantLayer.tsx sends the question with askGuest (city/lib/assistant/ask.ts) to app/api/v1/assistant/guest/route.ts. The server answers with read-only market tools from lib/assistant/guest.ts.
  • pickTowers in city/lib/assistant/answer.ts chooses which towers to light.
  • The command line that understands “buy SOL for 50” or “trade for me” is readAsk in city/lib/autopilot/read.ts.
Honest notes
  • Answers are information, not financial advice. Ryntra's guide has no tool that signs or sends money.
  • A few questions a minute per visitor; when the guide is busy, the city shows its own market read instead.
Chapter 03

The tower window: trading

Tap a tower, press Buy or Sell, sign once. You see the price and the fee before you sign.

A tower is also a shop. You walk in (tap it), see the price on the board, say how much you want and pay at the till (your wallet). Ryntra finds the cheapest way to make the swap through Jupiter, Solana's biggest swap router, and shows you the result and the fee before you agree. You sign once; the coins land in your own wallet.

Your steps
  1. Tap a tower (or find it in search).
  2. Choose Market, Limit or Stop, then Buy or Sell, and type how much you pay.
  3. Check “you get about …” and the fee, then Confirm in your wallet (the first time, Connect wallet).
  4. A limit order (“buy if the price drops to …”) waits in My orders until it fills or you cancel it.
How it fits together
The fee, in one line
Coin typeRyntra fee per trade
Memes and very new coins1 %
Trending, verified coins0.5 %
Tokenised stocks0.25 %
Big coins (SOL, BTC, ETH…)0.1 %
Stablecoins0
Under the hood
  • Window and ticket: city/components/trade/TradeSheet.tsx, city/components/trade/SpotTicket.tsx, orders in city/components/trade/MyOrders.tsx.
  • createEngine in city/lib/trade/engine.ts calls /api/solana/swap/quote, order and execute; the server prepares the swap in prepareSolanaSwap (lib/core/solana-swap/prepare.ts).
  • The fee table lives in exactly one place: FEE_POLICY_V2 in lib/core/fee-policy.ts. A verified coin counts as trending once its first pool is 7 days old; younger or unverified coins count as memes.
  • Limit orders go through Jupiter Trigger: reviewLimit, placeLimit, cancelLimit in city/lib/trade/limits.ts; the smallest order is $5.
  • Before you sign, the engine simulates the swap and refuses it if you would receive less than promised (assessSwapSimulationEffects, lib/solana/swap-effect-firewall.ts).
Honest notes
  • Prices move. The amount shown is an estimate inside a small slippage limit; the coin can be worth less tomorrow.
Chapter 04

Launching a coin: Meteora DBC → DAMM v2 → DLMM

Name, ticker, picture, one signature. Your coin gets a tower and grows on a Meteora curve.

Think of a new coin as a lemonade stand that starts on a ramp. At first the price is set by a simple rule: each buyer pushes the price a little higher up the ramp (the bonding curve, Meteora DBC). When enough people have bought — about $75,000 of market value — the stand “graduates”: it moves into a real market pool (Meteora DAMM v2), where its starting money is locked for ever so nobody can pull the rug. After that, Ryntra can add a ladder of buy orders under the price (Meteora DLMM). In the city you watch your tower rise the whole way.

Your steps
  1. Open Launch in the bottom bar (“Raise your tower”).
  2. Add a picture (take a photo or pick one), a name and a ticker.
  3. Press Review and choose bot protection: Protected (default) or Classic.
  4. Read Before you sign and press Launch — one signature. Your tower appears; share the link.
How it fits together
Who earns what
  • No launch fee — you pay only the Solana network fee.
  • Each trade on the curve pays 1 %. In the first minutes bots pay much more (an anti-sniper fee that falls to 1 %: over 10 minutes on Protected, 2 minutes on Classic).
  • That 1 % is split: creator 32 %, Ryntra 48 %, Meteora 20 % (the creator gets 40 % of what is left after Meteora's part).
Under the hood
  • Launch sheet: city/components/launch/LaunchSheet.tsx; the whole flow is runLaunch in city/lib/launch/flow.ts, which calls /api/v1/launch/build and /submit through httpEngine (city/lib/launch/engine.ts).
  • Curve presets (start, graduation at $75K, fees, anti-sniper): LAUNCH_PRESETS in lib/core/venues/meteora-dbc/presets.ts.
  • Graduation rules (DBC → DAMM v2, liquidity locked): lib/core/launch/constitution.ts.
  • Buy ladder after graduation: lib/core/venues/meteora-dlmm/floor.server.ts.
  • Who gets which share: lib/core/launch/revenue-rule.ts.
  • New coins have 1 billion tokens (Token-2022) and trade against USDC.
Honest notes
  • Most new coins never graduate. A launch is a start, not a promise of value.
  • The creator's fees are claimed with one signature from the coin's window.
Chapter 05

Your agent with a budget

Say what you want, sign one budget, walk away. The agent trades inside limits the blockchain enforces.

Imagine giving a helper a prepaid card with a daily limit, a list of shops where it works, and a rule that everything bought goes straight to your home. The helper can shop for you every day, but it can never spend more than the card allows, never shop elsewhere and never keep anything. And you can cut the card in half at any moment.

That is Ryntra's agent. The “card” is a Squads Smart Account on Solana with a spending policy. The limits are kept by the blockchain itself, not by a promise in our code.

Your steps
  1. Open Agents → Bots → Start (“Your agent”), or just say it: “trade for me”.
  2. Pick a coin and look at the plan; try it on history or practise without money first (chapter 6).
  3. Check the limits — ≤ 0.02 SOL a trade · 0.1 SOL a day · 30 days — and Sign once in your wallet.
  4. Every evening you get a short report; Stop anytime with one signature, and the rest comes back.
How it fits together
The limits, today on mainnet
  • Budget 0.1 SOL a day, at most 0.02 SOL a trade, for 30 days.
  • Only Jupiter routes are allowed; slippage at most 1 %.
  • Coins can only arrive in your wallet.
  • Autostop: the agent pauses after a 15 % loss in total, three losing buys in a row or three failed trades in a row.
  • Ryntra's fee is the normal trade fee from chapter 3 (0.1–1 % a trade), inside your limits.
Under the hood
  • Budget card: city/components/bots/AgentBudget.tsx; sentence → plan: readAsk in city/lib/autopilot/read.ts and city/lib/autopilot/plan.ts.
  • Budgets: BUDGET_PRESETS in lib/mandate/presets.ts; the policy itself (venues, per-trade, per-day, slippage, receiver, expiry) is built in lib/mandate/terms.ts and written as Squads Smart Account instructions in lib/mandate/ssa.ts.
  • Signing up and stopping: reviewEnable, reviewStop, relayMandate in lib/mandate/service.server.ts, routes under app/api/bots/mandate/.
  • The agent's turn: a cron every 5 minutes runs runAgentPass (lib/mandate/tick.server.ts); the strategy decides, the trade is checked, then signed by a key that lives in Google Cloud KMS (kmsKeySigner, lib/launch/signer/gcp-kms.server.ts). The agent never sees your key.
  • Every agent swap carries an extra instruction: “if the coins did not arrive in your account, cancel the whole trade” (receivedAtLeastInstruction, lib/mandate/result.ts).
  • Autostop rules: autostop in lib/mandate/strategy.ts.
  • Daily report: hourly cron, sent at your evening hour by runAgentReport (city/lib/autopilot/report-watch.server.ts).
Honest notes
  • Selling by the agent itself is coming. Today, when a strategy says “take profit”, the agent stops buying and you sell in one tap from its card.
  • Bigger budgets are coming; they open step by step.
  • An agent can lose money. Start small; the limits are there so a mistake stays small.
Chapter 06

Strategies

Proven, simple rules — regular buys, buy the dip, grid, early launches — tested on history first.

A strategy is the agent's recipe. You do not need to invent one: Ryntra has a short cookbook of simple recipes that traders have used for years. You choose the recipe in plain words, the agent follows it exactly — no mood, no guessing. Before cooking for real, you can see how the recipe would have done on history, and then practise with pretend money.

RecipeWhat it does, like you are 12
Regular buysBuy a little every day, whatever the price. Like saving pocket money weekly.
Buy the dipBuy only when the price has fallen by X %. Like waiting for a sale.
GridPlace steps of buys below the price, X % apart. Like a ladder that catches falls.
New launches that pass the checkEnter fresh coins only if they pass a safety filter.

Each recipe can have a take-profit (“sell when up X %”) and a stop (“get out when down X %”).

Your steps
  1. In Agents → Bots, pick a coin; Ryntra suggests a fitting plan (for example “trending up — an exit ladder fits”).
  2. Press On history to see a picture of the last days. It is a picture, not a promise.
  3. Press Practice first to run the plan with no money.
  4. When you are ready, Go live · sign once — or End practice.
How it fits together
Under the hood
  • The recipes and their bounds: PLAN_IDS (steady · dip · grid · early), planFrom, ruleOf in lib/mandate/plans.ts. Dip 3–50 %, grid steps 2–20 % with 2–10 levels, stop 3–15 % (never looser than the autostop), 1–24 buys a day.
  • The decision at trade time: decide in lib/mandate/strategy.ts. No AI model is asked at that moment; the rule decides. A strategy only decides whether now; it can never raise the size, the limits or change the venue.
  • History and practice: runPlan and paperRun in lib/mandate/backtest.ts, behind /api/bots/mandate/test. The history run counts fees, slippage and network costs.
  • The early-launch filter (earlyRefusal): at least $10K liquidity, $20K market cap, 100 holders, top 10 holders ≤ 30 %, developer ≤ 10 %, and not a serial launcher.
  • Labels in the city: city/lib/autopilot/words.ts.
Honest notes
  • Past results do not promise future ones.
  • The arena where agents compete with real budgets and the best strategies become templates is coming.
Chapter 07

Earn: savers and piers

Put coins to work in Jupiter Lend or a Meteora pool. The position stays in your wallet.

A saver is like a piggy bank at a bank that pays interest: you put in dollars (USDC) or SOL, others borrow them and pay you a small rate. Ryntra uses Jupiter Lend for the Dollar Saver. The SOL Saver turns SOL into staked SOL that grows a little every few days.

A pier is like owning a little stall in a busy market: you put two coins into a Meteora pool, and every time someone swaps through the pool, you get a slice of the fee. With one coin you can still open a pier — Ryntra swaps half for you in the same approval (Meteora Zap).

Your position always stays in your wallet; you can take it back anytime.

Your steps
  1. Open Earn. Tabs: Shelf (savers), Piers, Mine.
  2. On a card, read the live rate and press Start, Put to work or Add liquidity.
  3. Type an amount (for a pier, choose calm, balanced or dense) and approve once in your wallet.
  4. In Mine, watch your positions and press Take back when you want.
How it fits together
Under the hood
  • The window: city/components/earn/EarnWindow.tsx, cards Dollar.tsx, Saver.tsx, Piers.tsx.
  • Dollar Saver: jupiterLendAdapter in lib/earn/jupiter-adapter.ts; the server builds the deposit or withdrawal for your wallet (app/api/solana/earn/review, /submit) and checks the gates again before building and before sending (admitEarn, lib/earn/gate.ts).
  • Piers: city/lib/earn/pier.ts (styles, slippage, minimum 0.05 SOL), city/lib/earn/pier-gate.ts (who may add and how much).
  • One-coin pier: zap_in in lib/liquidity/studio/zap.ts — part of the SOL is swapped through Jupiter or the pool, whichever gives more, and the DLMM position opens; up to three transactions, one approval. If a zap lands only in part, Take the rest swaps the leftover back.
What Ryntra takes
  • Dollar Saver and pier positions: 0 % from Ryntra.
  • A swap step inside (SOL Saver, or the swap half of a Zap through Jupiter) pays the normal swap fee from chapter 3.
Honest notes
  • Rates change every minute; the number on the card is today's, not a promise.
  • Pools can lose value against simply holding (when prices move apart). The pier card shows the range you chose.
  • More savers (dollars under stocks, sell-higher / buy-lower orders) are coming.
Chapter 08

Money

Connect a wallet, top up, send, request a payment, see your portfolio — all in one window.

Your crypto wallet (Phantom, Solflare, Backpack or the Seeker phone) is your pocket. Ryntra never takes the money out of your pocket to keep it; it only shows you what is inside and prepares payments that you approve. The Money window is like a banking app laid over that pocket.

Your steps
  1. Tap Connect wallet and pick your wallet — no sign-up, no e-mail. (No wallet yet? Get Phantom.)
  2. Top up: copy your Solana address, or deposit from another network (Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, BNB).
  3. Send: type an address or a .skr name and the amount → Review → Confirm in wallet.
  4. Pay link, Activity and Portfolio: ask someone to pay you, see what happened and what you own.
How it fits together
Under the hood
  • The window and tabs: city/components/money/MoneyWindow.tsx, labels in city/lib/money/words.ts.
  • Wallets: detectWallets, connectWallet in lib/solana/wallet.client.ts (Wallet Standard). Proving it is your wallet is one message you sign — no transaction: proveWallet in city/lib/trade/proof.ts.
  • Send: city/lib/money/send.ts → /api/solana/transfer/plan, /settle, /recover; the plan is simulated before you sign. If your wallet adds its own safety checks, the engine accepts them (adopt).
  • Deposit from another network: lib/relay/deposit-address.ts (from $1).
  • Send to another network: city/lib/money/bridge.ts (USDC to an EVM address).
Honest notes
  • Top-up with a bank card is coming.
  • Ryntra never asks for your secret phrase. Anyone who does is not Ryntra.
Chapter 09

Partners and payouts

Share your link. You get part of Ryntra's fee from every friend, paid weekly in USDC.

When you bring a friend to a café and the café thanks you with a share of what your friend spends, everybody wins: your friend gets a discount, you get a thank-you, the café gets a new guest. Ryntra Partners works the same way, counted automatically and paid every week.

Your steps
  1. Open Menu → Partners and connect your wallet.
  2. Copy my link (it looks like ryntra.io/r/<your code>) and share it.
  3. Your friend opens the link and trades, launches or swaps in the city.
  4. Every week you receive your share in USDC to your wallet.
How it fits together
The terms
  • You: 40 % of Ryntra's fee from each invited person for their first 90 days, then 30 % for good.
  • Your friend: 10 % off Ryntra's fee.
  • Paid weekly in USDC; amounts under $5 roll over to the next week.
  • One level only — no pyramids.
Under the hood
  • The window and the /partners page: city/components/partners/PartnersWindow.tsx, city/components/partners/PartnersPage.tsx.
  • The link only remembers who invited you: [app/r/[code]/route.ts](<../../../app/r/[code]/route.ts>).
  • The terms in one place: PARTNER_TERMS in city/lib/partners/terms.ts.
  • The weekly statement: prepareStatement in lib/partner-payouts/week.ts — each fee-paying operation is credited exactly once, to the inviter recorded at that moment.
  • The payout: weeklyPass in lib/partner-payouts/payouts.server.ts turns the week into one Squads multisig proposal, signed by Ryntra's treasury; a cron prepares it (app/api/cron/partner-payouts). Referral abuse is filtered by lib/growth/referral-guard.ts.
Honest notes
  • What you earn depends only on what your friends really do; there is no promised amount.
Chapter 10

Phone and Seeker

The same city on Android, in the Solana dApp Store. Your phone's wallet signs.

The phone app is a window onto the same city. Whatever you do on the computer, you can do on the phone, with big buttons for your thumb. On a Solana Seeker phone, the wallet is built into the phone (Seed Vault), so signing is one tap with your fingerprint.

Your steps
  1. Install Ryntra from the Solana dApp Store (or open ryntra.io in the phone browser).
  2. Tap Connect wallet. The app opens your phone wallet through Mobile Wallet Adapter.
  3. Use the city as usual. Every money action pops up your wallet to approve.
  4. On Seeker your .skr name works in Send and in your profile.
How it fits together
Under the hood

Honest notes

Chapter 11

Safety, under the hood

Your keys stay with you. Limits live on the blockchain. Every transaction is checked before you sign.

You hold the keys to your own house. Ryntra is a helpful neighbour: it can carry your shopping to the door, but it has no key, and the door itself (the blockchain) refuses anything you did not allow. You can see all of this in one line on every screen: you sign once, you can stop anytime, the keys stay with you.

Your steps
  1. Read what you sign: the wallet shows every transaction before it goes.
  2. Start the agent small; the limits are there so a mistake stays small.
  3. Press Stop anytime — one signature, the rest comes back.
  4. Not sure about a transaction or a link? Paste it into Guard and press Check.
How it fits together
The rules, one by one
  1. Your keys stay in your wallet. Ryntra never sees or stores them; every money action is a transaction you sign.
  2. The agent's limits live on the blockchain. Per-trade and per-day limits, allowed venues and “coins go only to your wallet” are part of the Squads policy, so even a bug in our code cannot break them.
  3. The agent has its own key, with an expiry. It lives in Google Cloud KMS and can only act inside your budget; its access ends with the budget's term (30 days today).
  4. Every transaction is checked before it is sent. The engine simulates it and compares what you get with what was promised; if it does not match, it stops.
  5. “Money didn't reach you → the trade fails.” Agent trades carry an on-chain check that cancels the trade if the coins did not land in your account.
  6. Autostop and stop. The agent pauses itself after losses; you can stop it with one signature at any time.
  7. Coin names are data, not commands. Text from coin names, descriptions or messages is cleaned and never followed as an instruction by the AI.
  8. Guard. Paste any transaction or link into the Guard window and press Check to see what it would really do.
Under the hood
  • Pre-send check (Guard): checkWard in lib/ward/check.ts — the bytes, a simulation compared account by account, recipients and token powers.
  • Swap check: assessSwapSimulationEffects in lib/solana/swap-effect-firewall.ts fails closed when the simulated balances differ from the quote.
  • On-chain receive check: receivedAtLeastInstruction in lib/mandate/result.ts (Lighthouse AssertTokenAccount); the agent's signer refuses any trade without it (lib/mandate/signer.server.ts).
  • Policy limits: lib/mandate/terms.ts, lib/mandate/ssa.ts.
  • Text from the outside is cleaned by cleanData in lib/connector/tools.ts and marked “data, not instructions”.
Honest notes
  • Checks make mistakes rarer and smaller; they cannot make a trade profitable. Crypto prices can fall fast.
Chapter 12

The consultant and the agents

Ryntra at the top only advises. Agents in Bots do the money work — each with one job, its own budget and a stop.

Ryntra has two kinds of help, and it is always clear which one you are talking to.

The consultant is Ask Ryntra at the top of the city. Ask it anything about a coin or the market — «what is growing and why?» — and it answers in a few lines and lights up the towers. It only advises: it never trades, never signs, never holds a key. A few answers a day are free; after that each answer costs credits.

The agents are workers you hire in Agents → Bots. Each has one job: the Trader trades a coin for you, the Autopilot puts your money to work (savings, trading, pools), the Pool keeper looks after your Meteora pool, the Launcher launches your coin. Each works from its own budget in your wallet's vault, only inside the limits you signed, and stops with one signature. An agent is paid only by a fee inside its own trades — no credits.

Say «trade for me» or «look after my pool» to the consultant, and it does not answer with words: it shows you the agent who does that job and a Hire button.

Your steps
  1. Tap Ask Ryntra (on a computer: Ctrl K) and ask about any coin — the panel is marked Consultant.
  2. Want work done with your money? Say it: «trade for me», «look after my pool», «hire a trader».
  3. The line shows Hire · Trader · Agent (or the consultant shows This is a job for an agent) — press Hire.
  4. In the agent's own card read the budget and limits, then Sign once in your wallet; Stop anytime.
How it fits together
Under the hood
  • The two roles' words and the job reader: readHire in city/lib/crew/read.ts; the hire opens each agent's own card through city/lib/crew/hire.ts.
  • «Your agents» on top of Bots: city/components/crew/Crew.tsx; the consultant's card This is a job for an agent: city/components/crew/JobCard.tsx.
  • Whose model writes the consultant's words — OpenAI, Anthropic, or another through OpenRouter — is one switch of the deployment: lib/agent/providers.ts. Money is never a model's call: the engine's rules decide every trade.
  • Credits: lib/assistant/credits/prices.ts (an answer costs 2 credits, 1 credit = $0.001, 20 credits a day free).
Honest notes
  • The consultant can be wrong; its answers are information, not financial advice.
  • An agent can lose money. Start with a small budget; the limits are there so a mistake stays small.
  • An agent that is still being built shows Coming and cannot be hired yet.
